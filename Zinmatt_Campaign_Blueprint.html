01 — Executive Summary

Why this campaign works

India's digital marketing training market is a ₹3,000 Cr category where buyers have been burned repeatedly. A ₹0 teaching model removes the single largest objection before the conversation starts, so the ad only has to sell curiosity — not value.

01

The offer

A 90-day live Advanced Digital Marketing course with AI modules, one year of doubt support, internship and job assistance — taught at zero fee. Enrolment happens through the August 2 webinar.

02

The insight

The number one fear in this market is not quality, it is loss. "Paisa waste ho jayega." Zero price neutralises loss aversion, which is why free-offer lead gen consistently outperforms discount messaging in this category.

03

The mechanism

Meta Lead Generation objective with native Instant Forms, then WhatsApp community, then webinar. No landing page, no payment gate, the fewest possible steps between the scroll and the seat.

Say this in every ad FREE course. 90 days. Live classes. AI modules. Internship and job assistance. August 2 webinar. Limited seats.
Never say this in an ad No mention of the tools investment, fees, EMI or payment. That is disclosed transparently inside Day 1 of the webinar using the guitar analogy — the teaching is free, the instrument is yours.

04 — Our Strategy

A three-day sprint to a funded decision

The purpose of the first ₹2,750 is not volume. It is to identify which persona produces the cheapest registration, so that every rupee spent afterwards is spent with evidence.

A

Test wide, decide fast

Four audiences run simultaneously under one CBO campaign. Meta pushes spend toward whichever persona responds, so we learn in three days what ABO would take three weeks to reveal.

B

Two emotions per audience

Every ad set carries one pain-led ad and one gain-led ad. That way we learn the winning audience and the winning emotion inside the same test window.

C

Scale only what is proven

Winners are duplicated into a dedicated CBO scaling campaign at +20% increments. Losers are paused, not rescued. Retargeting picks up the leftover attention at the lowest cost in the account.

Three-day launch sequence

Budget deliberately ramps as confidence increases.

Day 1 — Launch ₹500 Learning phase

All four ad sets go live with eight ads. Half budget, no optimisation decisions. We let Meta exit the learning phase undisturbed and watch only CTR and CPM.

Day 2 — Optimise ₹1,000 First cuts

Any ad below 1% CTR after 1,000 impressions is paused. Attention shifts to the two cheapest ad sets. Full daily budget goes live.

Day 3 — Push ₹1,250 Urgency

Winners receive a 25% budget increase and urgency copy is swapped in — tomorrow's webinar, last remaining seats. Final WhatsApp reminder blast goes out.

August 2 — Webinar day Delivery

Live delivery to an expected 55–68 attendees from 136+ registrations, with the tools-cost disclosure handled inside Day 1 of the webinar.

Post-webinar — Scale Next cycle

Duplicate winning ad sets into a CBO scaling campaign, launch retargeting against video viewers and form abandoners, and repeat the sprint for the following webinar date.

Why CBO and not ABO for this test

On a three-day runway at ₹650 a day, ABO would split the budget into ₹160 slices — too little for any ad set to exit the learning phase. CBO pools the spend so a winning persona can emerge inside the test window. Once identified, that persona graduates into its own scaling campaign where ABO control becomes useful again.

What the account looks like after the test

Three campaigns running in parallel: this test campaign continuing at low spend for fresh learning, a CBO scaling campaign carrying the proven persona at increasing budget, and a retargeting campaign against video viewers, form openers who did not submit, and page engagers — historically the cheapest leads in the account.

Where the money goes

CBO decides distribution, but based on estimated CPL the expected split lands roughly at 32% to Tier-2 graduates, 26% to working professionals, 24% to women and 18% to business owners. We do not force this split — we observe it and then act on it.

Why a CPL this low is realistic A zero-fee offer inside a category where every competitor charges between ₹25,000 and ₹2.5 lakh produces unusually high click-through. Free-offer lead generation in Indian Tier-2 markets routinely lands between ₹12 and ₹35 per lead on Meta. Our forecast sits in the middle of that band, not at the edge.
